AlgoBot is an educational coding game designed to help kids discover the fundamentals of coding and algorithmic thinking through fun gameplay.
As children progress through the levels, they arrange commands in the correct order, guide their character to the goal, and build algorithms to solve different challenges.
With AlgoBot, kids can:
• Discover the fundamentals of coding logic
• Learn how to arrange commands in the correct order
• Develop algorithmic thinking skills
• Practice problem solving and planning
• Progress step by step through fun challenges
AlgoBot introduces fundamental coding concepts in a visual and interactive way, without requiring children to work with complex programming languages.
Think, plan, arrange the commands, and guide AlgoBot to the goal!
